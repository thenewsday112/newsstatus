#28. Milwaukee Brewers defeat Tampa Bay Rays to claim first World Series title

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The Milwaukee Brewers’ 57-season wait for a World Series title ended Wednesday as their victory over Tampa Bay completed one of the most dominant postseason runs in MLB history.

Once closer Trevor Megill induced a soft ground ball for the last out and their 4-1 win over the Rays in Game 5 was final, the Brewers rushed up the steps of their dugout and onto the playing field at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg, Florida. The celebration had been in the making for more than half a century.

“This is one of the greatest moments of my life,” Brewers shortstop Cooper Pratt said on Fox after the game. “Oh my gosh. It doesn’t even feel real. This is surreal.”

After having relocated from Seattle to Milwaukee and played for the first time as the Brewers in 1970, the team didn’t make the World Series until 1982, when it lost in seven games. It then waited 44 years to make its return to the Fall Classic.

For a franchise starved for big moments, this year’s team supplied one after another.

The Brewers’ 12-2 postseason record is among the best since the MLB postseason format expanded in 1995 to include wild-card teams. The Brewers’ .857 winning percentage was just off the percentages produced by the 1999 New York Yankees and the 2005 Chicago White Sox, which each went 11-1 en route to their own titles.

“I don’t even have words,” star pitcher Jacob Misiorowski, who threw 5 2/3 scoreless innings of the clinching game, told Fox after the Game 5 win.

Milwaukee is MLB’s smallest market. Its payroll ranked only 19th out of 30 teams. In 12 postseason games, it trailed nine times. But the Brewers won seven of those games by finding ways to prevail in multiple ways. Milwaukee hit seven home runs in a National League Championship Series sweep of the Los Angeles Dodgers yet took a 3-0 series lead on Tampa Bay while hitting just one homer. In Game 5, Milwaukee drove in runs using two singles, a sacrifice fly and a double.

Down to its final out in the bottom of the ninth inning, Tampa Bay got on the board when Chandler Simpson’s two-out double drove in Liam Hicks to trim the deficit to 4-1. With hard-throwing reliever Aaron Ashby waiting to relieve Megill in case he got into any more trouble, a soft grounder ended the game.

Catcher William Contreras earned Most Valuable Player honors after driving in four runs and hitting .381 during the World Series.

“I don’t have words to say,” Contreras said through a translator.

This was no fluke by a scrappy team that got hot at the right time. Milwaukee had the second-best record in baseball at July’s All-Star break and ultimately won an MLB-high and franchise-record 103 games. After it beat San Diego in the divisional round, it swept two-time reigning champion Los Angeles in the National League Championship Series. Even though the Dodgers’ payroll was more than double the Brewers’, Milwaukee scored 10 more runs over the sweep to secure a World Series berth against Tampa Bay.

The Rays were in some ways a mirror image of Milwaukee, another light-spending franchise looking to turn its regular-season success — the Rays had won the most games in the American League — into its first World Series victory.

“Best feeling ever,” Christian Yelich, the Brewers’ captain, said on the broadcast.

After one-run wins in the first two games of the World Series — including a Game 2 comeback in which Milwaukee fell behind 4-0 in the first inning — it won Game 3 in a 10-1 rout sparked by Christian Yelich’s stolen base in the fifth inning. When a challenge ruled Yelich safe at second base, Milwaukee proceeded to score eight runs with two outs, blowing the game open without ever hitting a home run.

Milwaukee’s championship run was possible thanks to a dependable core of players who spanned generations. Yelich, 34, and 33-year-old reliever Brandon Woodruff, whose save in Game 3 was his first appearance since July 4 because of injuries, were the only players remaining from a 2018 Brewers roster that was just one win away from that season’s World Series.

In the divisional round against San Diego, 20-year-old outfielder Jackson Chourio hit a late two-run homer that saved Milwaukee in its opening game of the postseason. Chourio then delivered a walk-off single to win the following game.

On the mound, Milwaukee constructed a feared rotation of arms starting with Misiorowski, the 24-year-old with blazing speed who made the All-Star Game as a rookie in 2025 after just five starts. For his encore, Misiorowski in 2026 led a pitching staff that allowed MLB’s second-lowest team ERA during the regular season. When he left games, a bullpen with arms just as nasty and hard-throwing as Misiorowski’s took over.

“This is just incredible,” Megill, on the mound for the final out, said after the win. “Ten-year-old me would be so proud.”

All of the pieces worked, all season. When the regular season ended, Milwaukee stood alone at the top of the standings.

And when the playoffs ended a month later, the Brewers were on top yet again. Their long wait for a title had ended.

Andrew Greif is a sports reporter for NBC News Digital.

#192. Billionaire Ambani cries tears of joy as Jio Platforms becomes the second most valued Indian firm

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Billionaire Mukesh Ambani has become the owner of the two most valued companies in India, after a stellar listing of Jio Platforms, the country's largest telecom operator and digital services firm.

The share of Jio Platforms continued to climb higher on Thursday, opening 3.3% higher a day after its market debut.

The Meta- and Google-backed company listed on Wednesday at a premium of over 25% to its IPO price, leading to a market capitalization of 13.05 trillion rupees ($137.1 billion).

Ambani's flagship company, Reliance Industries, also the most valued company in India, owns more than a 64% stake in Jio Platforms after the initial public offering.

Ahead of the listing, the patriarch of the Ambani family teared up as his three children shared the journey of building Jio and vowed to create value for investors over the next 50 years.

Analysts expect Jio's free cash flow to improve significantly as the funds from the initial public offering will be used to repay legacy telecom debt, and the company's 5G investment cycle is slowing down.

However, they cautioned that most of the value has been priced in after the listing gains, with the company now being valued on par with some of the global telecom giants.

With just 3% share dilution, Jio has wiped out nearly 300 billion rupees of debt, said Anurag Singh, founder and managing partner of U.S. based Ansid Capital, told CNBC. He added Jio's current market cap puts it in the league of American telecom giants such as AT&T and Verizon Communications.

AT&T has a market cap of $139 billion and Verizon of $179 billion, as per LSEG data.

In the next 10 years, Jio will "democratize every technology that can transform Indian lives," Akash Ambani, the eldest son of Mukesh and the managing director of Jio Platforms, said on Wednesday, ahead of the listing.

The 35-year-old scion listed out artificial intelligence, Cloud, connected devices, and "connectivity from even space," as some of the key areas for future growth.

The company's plans to launch satellite communications drew the ire of trillionaire Elon Musk, who blamed the Ambanis for the delays in the launch of Starlink in India earlier this month.

"Jio brings a rare scale to Indian equities," with a customer base of more than half a billion, operating margins of 52% and net leverage of 0.36 times, Vaqarjaved Khan, senior analyst at Indian broking firm Angel One, told CNBC.

He added that at a price-to-earnings of 34 times, much of the "quality" is priced in and future returns will be driven by improvement in average revenue per user and AI services.

Meanwhile, after Jio's listing pop, Reliance Group's market value of 30 trillion rupees has surpassed that of Tata Group's 25 trillion rupees, making Ambani family business the top conglomerate in the country, as per a Bloomberg report.

#10. Brewers beat the Rays to win the first World Series title in franchise history

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Milwaukee Brewers catcher William Contreras holds the MVP trophy after winning baseball's World Series against the Tampa Bay Rays, Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, in St. Petersburg, Fla. Morry Gash/AP hide caption

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla. — The small-ball Milwaukee Brewers playing in the smallest market in the majors are World Series champions for the first time.

Hey, Milwaukee: grab a brat, raise a beer and prepare for that parade.

Young ace Jacob Misiorowski threw 5 2/3 scoreless innings, rookie Cooper Pratt had two more hits and two RBIs, and Milwaukee beat the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 on Wednesday night to set off a long-awaited celebration among thousands of Brewers fans in attendance at Tropicana Field.

"It's the best feeling ever. All of our fans are here. I know the city's waited a long, long time for this," captain Christian Yelich said. "We're so happy to bring it home to them. I can't wait to get back there and celebrate."

World Series MVP William Contreras hit an RBI double and Misiorowski allowed just two hits before leaving the game because of a right calf cramp. He got a standing ovation from the Brew-Crew fans, who took over the section behind Milwaukee's dugout for the last three games.

The 22-year-old Pratt set a Series rookie record with six straight hits before his sacrifice fly in the sixth. Jackson Chourio set a five-game Series record with 11 hits.

"This is one of the greatest moments of my life. Oh my gosh. It doesn't even feel real," said Pratt, who made his major league debut in June. "This is surreal. It only took four months!"

The championship comes in the 58th season for a franchise that began as the Seattle Pilots in 1969 and moved to Milwaukee the following year. The Brewers' only previous trip to the World Series in 1982 ended in heartbreak when they blew a 3-1 lead in the sixth inning of Game 7 and lost 6-3 to the St. Louis Cardinals.

But this team displayed the toughness and resilience of 67-year-old manager Pat Murphy, who survived a heart attack in 2020 and was the NL Manager of the Year in his first two full seasons at the helm in 2024 and 2025.

"This group of guys right here, the 2026 team, will be remembered forever — and they deserve that because they were a team," Murphy said. "They're not worried about their own stats. No one cares about that. They care about coming together and winning tonight."

The Milwaukee Brewers celebrate after winning baseball's World Series against the Tampa Bay Rays, Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, in St. Petersburg, Fla. Ashley Landis/AP hide caption

With their flame-throwing, 24-year-old two-time All-Star on the mound, the Brewers rebounded from a heartbreaking, 4-3 extra-inning loss in Game 4 when they were three outs from a sweep in the 12th before a stellar bullpen finally faltered.

Pitching on four days' rest for only the fifth time in 34 starts this season, including the playoffs, The Miz had his longest outing since throwing 6 1/3 innings against the Cubs on Sept. 8. Leg cramps also forced him out of two outings earlier in the season.

"I don't even have words," Misiorowski said. "It's amazing, just watching my guys celebrate. And the city — I know they're going crazy in Milwaukee. It's awesome."

Misiorowski, who broke the World Series velocity record with a 103.5 mph fastball on the first pitch of the Series last Friday, opened with 12 straight four-seamers between 100.9-103 mph. He topped out at 103 twice among his 86 pitches, 58 strikes.

The right-hander didn't record a strikeout in the first three innings for the first time in his two-year career and finished with two, matching his career low. He was fired up after Yandy Díaz missed a cutter to end the fifth. The Miz pumped his fist twice and punched the air walking off the mound.

The NL Cy Young Award favorite allowed one run over five innings to get his first postseason win as a starter in a 3-2 victory in the opener. He was 2-0 with a 0.84 ERA in the Series.

JoJo Romero got two outs, Abner Uribe recorded two more and closer Trevor Megill pitched the last two innings, allowing an RBI double to Chandler Simpson with two outs in the ninth.

Megill retired Richie Palacios on a grounder to second to end it, threw his arms in the air and was mobbed by teammates near the mound while Brewers fans celebrated in the stands.

"Milwaukee has waited 44 years, and Milwaukee, you have your world championship," Brewers owner Mark Attanasio said from the postgame stage on the field.

Pratt, who was 4 for 5 with a tiebreaking solo homer in the 12th inning of Game 4, gave the Brewers a 1-0 lead with an RBI single against Rays ace Drew Rasmussen in the second. Pratt singled for his sixth straight hit in the Series in the fifth and scored on David Hamilton's single off Kevin Kelly.

Contreras hit an RBI double off Garrett Cleavinger in the sixth and Pratt added a sac fly to make it 4-0.

Pitching on three days' rest, Rasmussen went deeper than he did in Game 2, allowing one run and two hits in four innings. He had a 4-0 lead before he took the mound in Milwaukee on Saturday but couldn't hold it, lasting only 3 2/3 innings as the Brewers rallied for a 5-4 victory.

Rasmussen retired his first four batters before Brice Turang lined a double to left-center. Sal Frelick was hit by a pitch and Pratt ripped an RBI single to center but was thrown out in a well-executed rundown between first and second.

The Rays had runners on second and third with two outs in the fourth but Ben Williamson grounded out. Williamson, who was 1 for 15 in the postseason before his game-winning single in the 12th inning of Game 4, started over the lefty-swinging Palacios.

Kelly replaced Rasmussen to start the fifth and allowed consecutive one-out singles to Pratt, Garrett Mitchell and Hamilton.

The teams combined to hit just four homers in the Series, three for Milwaukee. The Brewers scored a major league-low 32.1% of their runs on homers and Tampa Bay was second-lowest at 34.4%, according to the Elias Sports Bureau.

The Brewers led the majors with 103 wins in the regular season and went 11-2 in the postseason, including seven comeback wins and a sweep of the mighty Dodgers in the NLCS.

Milwaukee's only other MLB championship was won in 1957 by the Braves, who moved to Atlanta for the 1966 season.

Murphy's team finished what Harvey Kuenn's star-studded crew couldn't accomplish 44 years ago as the AL champions. Harvey's Wallbangers had five Hall of Famers: Robin Yount, Paul Molitor, Ted Simmons, Don Sutton and Rollie Fingers, who missed the postseason with a forearm injury. They also had Hall of Fame broadcaster Bob Uecker in the booth.

The Rays, an expansion team that debuted in 1998, dropped to 0-3 in the World Series after defeats in 2008 and 2020. The only other clubs to lose in their first three Series appearances were the Brooklyn Dodgers (0-7) and Detroit Tigers (0-4), according to the Elias Sports Bureau.

"They're banged-up and hurting right now, but just grateful for the way they carried themselves all season long, winning a bunch of games. It's as close-knit of a group as I've ever been around," Tampa Bay manager Kevin Cash said.

This might have been the last Major League Baseball game for an extended period.

The Milwaukee Brewers celebrate after winning baseball's World Series against the Tampa Bay Rays, Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, in St. Petersburg, Fla. Ashley Landis/AP hide caption

The Brewers won the World Series at a time when owners are pushing for a salary cap from the players' association because they maintain small-market teams can't regularly succeed. A management lockout is likely to start Dec. 2 and could threaten the 2027 season.

This was the first Series since 1991 between teams in the bottom half of payrolls. The Brewers ranked 19th as of Aug. 31 and Rays were 25th.

The Rays were trying to become the first team to fall behind 3-0 in a World Series and force a sixth game. Of the 26 teams that lost the first three games, 21 were swept and Tampa Bay became the fifth to lose in Game 5.

#103. Google has a plan to keep ads alive even when AI agents do the shopping

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Google has a pitch for advertisers worried AI agents will make Search obsolete: Let the bots do the shopping legwork, but leave people to compare brands, prices, perks — and sponsored offers.

"When users shop, and that's important, there's a part of the journey they don't like, the grunt work, which is frankly fantastic to automate with AI," Philipp Schindler, Google's chief business officer, said on Alphabet's earnings call on Tuesday. "But we also want to enhance, not just automate away the discovery experience that people frankly love in so many cases."He said users do not always want the cheapest option; they also weigh brand loyalty and customer service. Google believes that this decision-making process gives it space to build ads into agentic shopping.

He shared an example of how that might work: "Here's the insurance product we found, and here's another strong option, one of which is a higher premium, maybe a lower deductible, and they are currently offering a very special discount."

Schindler added that there is almost never a single perfect solution for any shopping decision, which makes Google "confident" that there is room for advertising even in agentic search.

The comments offer a clearer explanation of Google's business model for agentic search, which has presented a challenge for its core ads business. On Tuesday, the company said that Google's AI mode has led to more searches than ever, but it's not making money at the same rate. It's a major concern among investors, who have long argued that AI chatbots and agents will threaten Google's search business.

Google has been signaling for months that it wants to solve that problem without simply cramming old-style ads into a chatbot. Last quarter, Google said it was testing ideas like showing offers during travel planning and clearly marked sponsored links inside AI-generated lists.

Alphabet reported third-quarter revenue of $127.58 billion, narrowly below the $127.97 billion analysts expected. Earnings per share came in at $2.12, versus roughly $3.01 expected, largely because of an $8.2 billion unrealized loss on its SpaceX investment. Shares fell about 10% after hours.

#137. Over 41,000 U.S. Homeland Security officials' details apparently exposed after massive data breach

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The identities of over 41,000 officials from the Department of Homeland Security have allegedly been exposed in a massive data breach by a hacktivist group calling itself the Department of Peace.

Late Wednesday stateside, the database was posted on Distributed Denial of Secrets, a nonprofit dedicated to news leaks, with claims that it was obtained from an Okta single sign-on service used by the DHS.

It appears to reveal employee names, offices, contact information and assignments, and is searchable.

Okta didn't immediately respond to a request for comment.

DDoS said that "this is not a comprehensive list of all DHS employees," quoting cofounder Emma Best saying, "Rather than an indiscriminate directory of every DHS employee, Icepick identifies many of the architects and drivers of DHS policy and operations."

DHS didn't immediately respond to a request for comment. The agency told the Washington Post that it was "aware of a potential breach" and was "working to address the situation swiftly and effectively."

The searchable database appears to include employees from Immigration and Customs Enforcement, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services, Customs and Border Protection and the DHS headquarters.

The hacker group said the leak was carried out in response to DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin reportedly saying on July 17 that ICE will be "turning up the heat on the streets" by intensifying immigration arrests and traffic stops.

In U.S. President Donald Trump's second term, the backlash to his efforts to intensify arrests and deportations of suspected illegal immigrants has increased as ICE agents have been documented using physical force during operations.

DHS completed 980,000 deportations in the first 18 months of his term, according to the Migration Policy Institute.
